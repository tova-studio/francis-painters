About

Francis Painters Limited, Tradesman Decorator

Established in 1998, Francis Painters is a Christchurch painting and decorating business working on residential and commercial properties.

Beyond interior and exterior painting, Francis Painters takes on many of the tradesman tasks that go with it. These include plastering, crack repairs, weatherboard replacement, reglazing, roofing repairs and specialist protective coatings.
